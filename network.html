About the Network

Connecting People Across Sensor Intelligence

The Sensor Intelligence Lab & Network brings together collaborators working across sensor physics, nanotechnology, photonics, biomedical engineering, environmental monitoring, AI/ML, data modelling, and translational sensor systems.

Materials & Interfaces

Nanomaterials, interfaces, photonics, electrochemical and bioinspired sensing.

Health & Biology

Breathomics, biomedical devices, biosensors, diagnostics, and precision health.

Environment & Society

Environmental sensing, wastewater monitoring, One Health, and sustainable sensors.

AI & Translation

Sensor data modelling, AI/ML, intelligent analytics, deployment, and partnerships.
