SINk-2026

Sensor Intelligence Network Conference 2026

Sustainable Conference | Primarily Online | Global Participation

Tentative Dates: 24–27 September 2026

From sensing materials to intelligent decisions

SINk-2026 is a proposed international forum of the Sensor Intelligence Lab and Sensor Intelligence Network. The conference aims to bring together researchers, students, educators, clinicians, engineers, industry partners, and early-stage innovators working across sensors and sensor intelligence.

The conference will focus on how sensors can move beyond passive detection toward intelligent, adaptive, sustainable, interpretable, and decision-oriented systems. It will encourage dialogue between theoretical, simulation-based, experimental, applied, and translational approaches.

Key Areas and Broad Themes

  • Physics, chemistry, and engineering for sensors
  • Sensor materials, interfaces, and transduction mechanisms
  • Sensors for biology, medicine, healthcare, and breathomics
  • Sensors for environment, climate, One Health, and sustainability
  • Sensors for industries, deployment, and real-world challenges
  • Computation, AI/ML, simulations, and sensor intelligence
  • Digital twins, explainable AI, IoT, and edge intelligence
  • Translational research, innovation, and industry partnerships
  • Challenges, limitations, and practical solutions in sensors
  • Education, training, and future skills in sensor intelligence

Format

SINk-2026 is planned as a sustainable, primarily online conference with maximum global participation. The program may include keynote lectures, invited talks, contributed presentations, poster sessions, panel discussions, student sessions, training activities, and networking opportunities.

Student and Outreach Activities

The conference will include special activities for undergraduate students and young learners. Students may present ideas, concepts, problem statements, prototypes, or creative visions related to sensors and intelligence, even if they are not yet formal research projects.

School-level outreach activities may also be included to introduce basic concepts of sensors, measurement, data, and intelligent decision-making.

Introductory Certificate Course

An introductory training component on Sensor Intelligence will be included. Participants who attend all required conference events and training sessions will receive a Certificate of Completion for the Introductory Course on Sensor Intelligence.

Institutional Partnership

SINk-2026 welcomes universities, research institutes, academic centers, laboratories, professional bodies, innovation hubs, and industry-linked institutions interested in partnering with the event.

Institutions interested in supporting SINk-2026 may join as Institutional Partners, contributing through thematic sessions, student activities, invited lectures, outreach, training modules, or collaborative networking.
