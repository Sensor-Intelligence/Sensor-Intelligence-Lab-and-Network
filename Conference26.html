SINk-2026

Sensor Intelligence Network Conference 2026

Hybrid Conference | Inaugural Session in Thailand and Online | Global Participation

Dates: 24–27 September 2026

Final Submission Deadline: 25 August 2026

Mode: Hybrid

Inaugural Session: Mahidol University, Nakhon Sawan Campus, Thailand and Online

From sensing materials to intelligent decisions

SINk-2026 is an international forum of the Sensor Intelligence Lab and Sensor Intelligence Network. The conference aims to bring together researchers, students, educators, clinicians, engineers, industry partners, and early-stage innovators working across sensors and sensor intelligence.

The conference will focus on how sensors can move beyond passive detection toward intelligent, adaptive, sustainable, interpretable, and decision-oriented systems. It will encourage dialogue between theoretical, simulation-based, experimental, applied, and translational approaches.

Key Areas and Broad Themes

  • Physics, chemistry, and engineering for sensors
  • Sensor materials, interfaces, and transduction mechanisms
  • Sensors for biology, medicine, healthcare, and breathomics
  • Sensors for environment, climate, One Health, and sustainability
  • Sensors for industries, deployment, and real-world challenges
  • Computation, AI/ML, simulations, and sensor intelligence
  • Digital twins, explainable AI, IoT, and edge intelligence
  • Translational research, innovation, and industry partnerships
  • Challenges, limitations, and practical solutions in sensors
  • Education, training, and future skills in sensor intelligence

Certificate Course and Student Idea Competition

SINk-2026 will include a Level-1 Introductory Certificate Course on Sensor Intelligence for undergraduate students, postgraduate students, and school students.

The course will introduce students to the basic concepts of sensors, measurement, data, intelligence, AI/ML, sustainability, and decision-making in sensing systems.

A student idea competition will also be organised for undergraduate and school-level participants. Students may present ideas, concepts, problem statements, prototypes, or creative visions related to sensors and intelligence, even if they are not yet formal research projects.

Participants who attend the required course sessions and training activities will receive a Certificate of Completion for the Level-1 Introductory Course on Sensor Intelligence.
